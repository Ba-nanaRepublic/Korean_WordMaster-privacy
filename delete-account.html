How to request deletion

Email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will reply and complete your request within 30 days.

What this app stores

  • Learning data (memorized words, bookmarks, quiz results, goals, settings): stored only on your device. We do not have a server and cannot see it.
  • Google sign-in (optional): your name, email and photo are shown inside the app only. They are not sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Purchases: payments are processed by Google Play. The app only keeps a local "subscribed or not" flag.
  • Analytics, crash and ad data: collected by Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob (device identifiers, app usage, crash logs). It is not linked to a user account.

Delete the data yourself (no email needed)

  1. Sign out: open the app → Settings → Logout.
  2. Reset learning progress: Settings → Reset words.
  3. Delete everything on the device: uninstall the app, or Android Settings → Apps → this app → Storage → Clear data.
  4. Remove ad identifier data: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

Subscriptions

Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. To cancel, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → this app → Cancel subscription.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all data described above that is associated with you and that we are able to identify.
  • Kept: analytics, crash and ad data already processed by Google (Firebase / AdMob) follow Google's own retention periods, and purchase records are kept by Google Play according to its policies and applicable law. We do not retain extra copies.
